NAVIGATION

Connection

Not connected

Cable mode needs Chrome or Edge. The first cable connection must be started with the mouse.

Wi-Fi for the ESP32

Auto-connect sends the network to the ESP32 over the cable, or over its own JoyLink Wi-Fi when it has no working network. It remembers the last one. Passwords are stored in this browser only.

Finger mouse

Waiting for the ESP32…

Move the finger mouse over a button to select it, then press the joystick to click. Use Rotate/Mirror until the cursor moves the way your finger does.

Joystick tuning

Hands off the stick when recalibrating. A move counts only when you push past the ring, hold briefly, and let go.

Text

Typing codes

Flick the stick (push, release) U / D / L / R. Press the button = space. In typing mode, press with half a code entered to cancel it. LL + direction picks a suggestion shown in the top bar. RR + two flicks opens the rare letters.
